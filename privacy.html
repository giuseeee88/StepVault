Privacy Policy & GDPR Notice

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Controller (Art. 13(1)(a) GDPR)

The Data Controller for StepVault is the StepVault Developer, reachable for any privacy or GDPR request at giuseppecaut57@gmail.com.

2. Local-Only Extension Processing (Privacy by Design — Art. 25 GDPR)

  • 100% On-Device Vault: All screenshots, recorded steps, DOM labels, sanitized URLs, PII redaction boxes, and exported files (PDF, HTML, Markdown, ZIP, JSON) are processed and stored exclusively on your device inside your browser's local IndexedDB storage.
  • Zero Cloud Collection: We do not collect, transmit, access, or store your browsing history, screenshots, form inputs, or guide contents on any server. Password, OTP, CVV, and API token fields are masked at source (••••••••), and sensitive URL query tokens are automatically redacted.
  • Free Tier Network Isolation: StepVault Free performs zero external network requests.

3. Purposes, Legal Bases & External Recipients (Art. 6 & Art. 13 GDPR)

  • Pro Checkout & Invoicing (Art. 6(1)(b) & 6(1)(c) GDPR): Purchases of StepVault Pro are processed by our Merchant of Record, Polar Software Inc. (Polar.sh), which independently handles payment details, billing email, and statutory VAT/tax compliance.
  • Pro License Activation & Validation (Art. 6(1)(b) & 6(1)(f) GDPR): When you activate or revalidate a Pro license key, the extension connects over HTTPS solely to api.polar.sh (enforced by a strict browser Content Security Policy), sending only the license key string, organization ID, activation ID, and generic extension label to enforce the 3-browser activation limit.
  • Website Hosting & Support (Art. 6(1)(b) & 6(1)(f) GDPR): This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.), which processes standard technical HTTP logs (IP address) for security and delivery. Support emails sent voluntarily to giuseppecaut57@gmail.com are used solely to reply to your inquiry.

4. Cookies, Local Storage & Zero Trackers (ePrivacy Art. 5(3))

Neither the StepVault extension nor this website uses profiling cookies, analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or external font CDNs (only local system fonts are used, preventing IP leaks). On this website, a technical localStorage preference (stepvault_site_lang) is saved only if you explicitly click the EN/IT language switcher.

5. International Transfers & Data Retention (Art. 13(2)(a) & Art. 44–49 GDPR)

Where Polar Software Inc. or GitHub Inc. process technical or billing data in the United States, transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and/or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs, Art. 46 GDPR). Local guides remain on your device until you delete them (via the in-app Privacy & GDPR → Delete All Data button or by uninstalling the extension). Billing records are retained by Polar.sh for the statutory tax period; support emails are deleted within 12 months.

6. Your GDPR Rights & Supervisory Authority (Art. 15–22 & Art. 77 GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21). For local guides, you can exercise instant portability (JSON/ZIP export) and instant erasure directly inside StepVault's Privacy & GDPR Controls panel. For license or support data, contact giuseppecaut57@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national EU Data Protection Authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali — garanteprivacy.it).

7. User Responsibility When Recording Third-Party Data

When documenting workflows that display personal data of clients, employees, or third parties, you act as the independent controller of the exported files. Always review screenshots and apply StepVault's irreversible pixelation/blackout tools before sharing exported PDF, HTML, or Markdown guides externally.
