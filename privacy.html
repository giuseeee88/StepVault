Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Local Data Processing

StepVault is a local-first browser extension. All screenshots, recorded steps, text descriptions, PII detections, and exported files (PDF, HTML, Markdown, ZIP) are processed and stored exclusively on your device inside your browser's local IndexedDB storage.

Zero Data Collection

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your browsing history, screenshots, or guide contents on external servers.
  • We do not embed analytics, trackers, or third-party advertising scripts.

License Key Verification

Checkout and invoicing for StepVault Pro are handled by our Merchant of Record, Polar.sh. When activating a Pro license key, only the license key string and a device label are sent to Polar's API to validate activation.
