How StepVault Works

From live browser clicks to polished documentation in 4 steps

No video editing, no manual screenshot cropping, and no uploading confidential client screens to third-party servers.

1

Hit Record

Open the StepVault popup on any web app, choose English or Italian step descriptions, and perform your procedure as usual.

2

Auto-Capture & Mask

Clicks, form inputs, and dropdowns are captured across tabs. Passwords and API tokens are masked at source (••••••••) while a floating Shadow-DOM HUD tracks progress without appearing in your shots.

3

Polish & Redact

Open the full-tab Local Editor to reorder steps, add Tip / Warning / Info callouts, switch between Smart Zoom Crop and Full Viewport, and pixelate sensitive areas.

4

Flatten & Share

Redactions are permanently baked into exported JPEG pixels so hidden data can never be recovered. Export to branded PDF, offline HTML, ZIP, or Markdown in one click.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is €19 really a one-time payment, with no subscription?

Yes. You pay €19 once and receive a permanent Lifetime License key. There are no monthly renewals, per-guide limits, or cloud storage fees — because all processing runs on your own machine.

Do my screenshots or client data ever touch an external server?

Never. Screenshots, DOM labels, PII scans, and PDF/HTML/ZIP exports are processed 100% locally inside your browser and stored in local IndexedDB. The only network request StepVault ever makes is verifying your license key string with Polar.sh.

Can someone remove the blur or recover redacted text from exported files?

No. Before exporting to PDF, HTML, Markdown, or ZIP, StepVault permanently flattens pixelation and solid blackout boxes directly into the JPEG image bitmap. The underlying sensitive pixels are overwritten and cannot be reversed.

How many devices can I activate, and what if I buy a new computer?

Each Pro license key lets you activate up to 3 browser instances simultaneously (for example, work desktop, laptop, and Firefox). You can click "Deactivate on this browser" anytime inside the extension to free up a slot for a new machine, and your key remains valid for life.

Does StepVault Pro work offline?

Yes. Recording, smart cropping, PII detection, editing, and exporting all work completely offline. Once your license is activated, StepVault keeps Pro unlocked during offline travel or isolated network sessions.

Can I get a business VAT invoice, and what is the refund policy?

Checkout is handled by Polar.sh (our Merchant of Record), which automatically provides tax/VAT-compliant invoices for companies and freelancers worldwide. If StepVault Pro doesn't fit your workflow, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.
